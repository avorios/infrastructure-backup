## Product: Tether

Tether is an **intentional community platform** — it places people into real-world connection spaces (gatherings, small groups, 1:1) based on the Tether Alignment Measure (TAM), an 11-question orientation that captures how someone wants to show up, not who they are.

**It is NOT** a dating app, personality test, matching engine, or social network with feeds.
**It IS** an environment engineered for meaningful encounter — friendship, dating, teams, families.

**Key concepts:** TAM onboarding flow, tag-based placement engine, curated spaces/gatherings, life-stage safety boundaries, fragile space detection, AI that mirrors (never labels).

**Tech stack:** React 18 + TypeScript + Vite 5, Tailwind + shadcn/ui, Supabase (auth, DB, edge functions), Capacitor for mobile.

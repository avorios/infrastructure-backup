The Problem

Schools are flying blind on student safety

⚠

No visibility during class time

Teachers manage 25+ students on devices with zero insight into what's on their screens. Harmful content goes undetected until it's too late.

🔒

Privacy concerns block adoption

Existing tools monitor 24/7 with no boundaries. Parents distrust always-on surveillance. Schools face FERPA/COPPA compliance risks.

📈

Fragmented, outdated tooling

Districts juggle 3-5 separate tools for monitoring, filtering, wellness, and SIS integration. Legacy software built on decade-old architectures.

Our Solution

Session-based monitoring that respects privacy

🟢

Active Session Only

Monitoring activates only during class sessions. No 24/7 surveillance. Full audit trail of every capture.

🤖

AI-Powered Safety

Claude AI detects harmful content, cyberbullying patterns, and student wellness signals in real-time.

🎯

Smart Filtering

Context-aware web filtering that adapts to lesson plans. Auto-switches policies between school and home.

👥

Role-Based Access

Six distinct roles (teacher, admin, parent, student, IT, super admin) each see only what they need.

🛡

FERPA/COPPA Compliant

Built from day one for education privacy laws. Data minimization, parental consent flows, secure storage.

🔗

SIS Integration

Plug into Google Classroom, Clever, ClassLink, and PowerSchool. Zero manual class roster management.
